# Design Context

### Users
- **Administrators**: Manage courses, departments, academic programmes, users, and sessions. They need high information density, clear readability, easy access to filters, and streamlined CRUD operations.
- **Students**: Register for courses, download notes/past questions, view academic results. They need a clean, structured, and guiding registration wizard.
- **Lecturers**: Manage assigned courses, upload grades, and post resource materials. They need a straightforward, clutter-free flow.

### Brand Personality
- Professional, clean, and modern.
- Refined, trustworthy, and organized.
- Cohesive layout prioritizing high readability, a neutral base with clear accent details, and purposeful motion.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Modern typography and clean visual rhythm.
- Avoids generic AI templates, heavy card nesting, decorative gradients, or glassmorphic glow.
- Balanced white space, light borders, subtle hover transitions, and clean UI components (shadcn/ui style).

### Design Principles
1. **Information Hierarchy**: Prioritize readability and clean layout structure. Avoid excessive cards; use space, alignment, and light separators to define groups.
2. **Progressive Disclosure**: Present essential information first. Advanced controls, settings, and forms are kept in clean sidebars, tabs, or disclosures.
3. **Fluid and Adaptive Spacing**: Spacing should feel intentional, adapting beautifully to desktop and mobile environments.
4. **Responsive Integrity**: Adapt layout components contextually rather than shrinking them to tiny viewports.
